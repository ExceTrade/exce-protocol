Part 1

The path of a record

Every fact earns its slot by clearing four stages. Skip one and the slot stays blank.

i.

The team states it

The team hands over a fact: an address, a handle, a network. Until that happens, the record reads absent.

Nothing is guessed to fill a gap.
ii.

It gets dated

Every record carries the date it was last looked at, so an old answer shows its age instead of posing as fresh.

iii.

The slot lights up

A stated record alone turns a slot live: a link opens, an address can be copied. Absent and unconfirmed ones stay flat and quiet.

iv.

A doubt switches it off

When a statement is questioned, the record drops to unconfirmed and the slot goes still again until someone restates it.
